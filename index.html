USE AI WISELY

Responsible AI

AI is powerful, but students should use it as a learning assistant rather than a replacement for their own thinking.

✅ DO

  • 📚 Use AI to understand difficult concepts.
  • 🧠 Think about and verify AI answers.
  • ✍️ Add your own ideas and creativity.
  • 🔎 Check important information.
  • 🎓 Use AI to learn, not simply copy.

❌ DON'T

  • 🚫 Copy AI answers without understanding them.
  • 🚫 Depend completely on AI.
  • 🚫 Assume every AI answer is correct.
  • 🚫 Share private or sensitive information.
  • 🚫 Use AI to cheat.
